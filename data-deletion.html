User Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: October 2026

GDA Systems respects your privacy and gives you full control over the personal data we store in connection with our CRM platform and connected services.

If you connected your Facebook account or submitted a form through Facebook/Meta, you have the right to request that your personal information be permanently removed from our databases.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request the deletion of your personal data, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to: gd.ai.systems@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: "Data Deletion Request"
  3. In the body of the email, please include:
    • Your full name
    • The email address or phone number associated with your account or submission
    • (Optional) Your Facebook user profile link if applicable

What Happens Next?

  • Upon receiving your request, our team will locate and permanently purge your records (contact information, activity history, and messages) from our CRM and cloud database.
  • We will send you a confirmation email within 30 days confirming that your data has been completely erased.

Removing App Access via Facebook

You can also revoke our application's access to your Facebook account at any time:

  1. Go to your Facebook Profile's Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. Click on Apps and Websites in the left menu.
  3. Find GDA Systems in the list.
  4. Click Remove.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding your data, please contact us at gd.ai.systems@gmail.com.
